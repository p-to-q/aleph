# Open Questions

This file is for real uncertainty. Do not let unresolved questions become product claims or fixture behavior become evidence.

## First-round questions to settle

### 0. Permanent frontend path

Settled:

- `web/` is the active Next.js app.
- `apps/web/` is archived reference material.

### 1. First PR0-conformant research-grade model adapter

Status: **product adapters exist; the research-grade adapter/archive remains open**.

Settled implementation:

- the product has fixture, hosted black-box, and local MLX routes;
- PR #100 fixed the `monotone()` frontier mutation bug and made displayed candidates derive from
  observations;
- the historical exported MLX frontier remains invalid performance evidence because it predates that
  repair and lacks a complete archive, lineage, fixed metric behavior, and research receipt.

Still open: implement the first pinned, PR0-conformant local open-weight search adapter with separate
proposal/evaluation roles, exact tokenizer accounting, an append-only archive, fresh confirmation,
the six-account resource ledger, and independent replay. Regenerate public evidence rather than
rewriting the historical export.

### 2. Default metric

Status: **superseded as a single-metric question by the governing PR0 research contract**.

Settled boundary:

- no undocumented composite is the scientific default;
- every PR0-conformant or paper-facing run must use a versioned metric manifest with distinct
  fidelity/residual, rerun reliability,
  human surface readability, human target linkage, nuisance stability, and copy-channel fields where
  applicable;
- missing components remain `unknown`; they are not imputed into a favorable scalar;
- the confirmed scientific view is a multidimensional partial order, while a scalar or projection may
  be used only by a separately named scheduler or UI rule.

Still open: the primary distortion family for each target stratum, calibration thresholds, interval
construction, and the preregistered projection used by a user-facing slider.

### 3. Leakage score

Status: **superseded by PR0's typed provenance and recoverability contract**.

Settled boundary:

- the existing n-gram value is a **surface-copy proxy**, not a general leakage or provenance score;
- surface copy, semantic recovery, deterministic encoding, public reference, corpus trace, training
  influence, extractability, association effect, self-citation, and search-trace integrity are
  separate typed channels;
- a finite, versioned reconstructor suite may support `tested-noncopy` only relative to its declared
  code, side information, calibration, thresholds, and budget;
- a scalar threshold-normalized maximum may schedule search, but the full channel vector remains the
  scientific artifact.

Still open: the first frozen probe-suite version, its channel-specific nulls and power targets, and
the migration of the legacy `leakage` wire field. Universal “non-leaking” language is prohibited.

### 4. Search strategy

Status: **partially settled by PR0; the Aleph algorithm remains open**.

Options:

- heuristic candidate generation;
- black-box evolutionary search;
- MiniPrompt/ACR reproduction and audited extension;
- ARCA/GCG-style fixed-length raw-coordinate optimization;
- soft-prompt route later.

Settled boundary: MiniPrompt/ACR is the mandatory closest white-box empirical baseline and Prompting
Complexity is the direct formal neighbor. ARCA/GCG are lower-level raw comparators. The historical MLX
frontier export supplies no valid performance point until repaired and regenerated. The open question
is whether compression continuation with residual repair improves discovery at matched work after all
methods pass through one evaluator, archive, and confirmation boundary.

### 5. Research mainline after the first release

Options:

- Aleph as a product workbench first;
- Aleph as a benchmark/reporting instrument first;
- Aleph as a prompt-search engine first;
- hybrid, with the workbench as the public face and deeper search routes as adapters.

Maintainer leaning: keep the workbench as the public identity. Benchmarking and deeper search matter, but they should strengthen the workbench rather than replace it.

### 6. UI emphasis

Options:

- research console;
- public product page;
- hybrid landing-plus-console.

Maintainer leaning: build the console first. A landing page can narrate the console later.

### 7. Human-readable prompt coordinates vs raw coordinates

Status: **partially settled by PR0**.

Settled boundary:

- readable and raw coordinates are two required, separately certified views over one append-only
  observed archive;
- raw coordinates are a mandatory comparator now, not deferred future work;
- both views share target-model evaluation metrics, but not legal mutation constraints, human
  eligibility predicates, or certification labels;
- the product may default to the readable view, while the paper must report the readable-versus-raw
  gap and preserve raw baselines such as MiniPrompt/ARCA/GCG.

Still open: the exact raw-token legality contract, the human surface/link protocol, and the UI
projection that preserves this distinction without calling raw coordinates readable prompts.

### 8. Persistence

Options:

- no persistence;
- local JSON export/import;
- file-backed runs;
- database.

Maintainer leaning: JSON export/import before database.

### 9. Computer-science paper identity

Options:

- program-synthesis/search paper with model-relative coordinates as the synthesis object;
- measurement/benchmark paper with a new readable-coordinate construct and protocol;
- formal-methods paper around certificates and finite guarantees;
- HCI/systems paper around checkable compression paths;
- a staged combination, with one primary claim and the others as supporting evaluations.

Maintainer leaning: keep the primary domain in computer science—program synthesis, search,
verification, benchmark science, reproducible systems, and human checkability. Mathematical landmark
problems should provide difficult instances, falsification pressure, and proof artifacts. They should
not turn the first paper into a survey of company math demonstrations or an untestable claim of
general mathematical discovery.

### 10. Description, discovery, and verification states

Open questions:

- What minimum artifact makes a candidate a **description** rather than model prose?
- What literature search, baseline recovery, timestamp, and expert review are required before Aleph
  labels a result a **discovery**?
- Which executable predicate, formal statement, checker, assumptions, and trusted base define a
  **verification** event?
- Can one candidate occupy multiple states without collapsing their evidence?
- How are negative review, counterexamples, supersession, and withdrawal preserved append-only?

Maintainer leaning: represent these as distinct evidence states with separate receipts. Description
does not imply novelty; novelty does not imply correctness; and a formal certificate verifies only its
declared statement and environment.

### 11. Lean and finite-enumerator continuation

Status: **first bootstrap merged; the general finite theory remains open**.

Merged PR #103 supplies `bounded-coordinate-v0`: a standard-library exhaustive finite enumerator,
an independent Python reconstruction, and a pinned Lean project for concrete coverage, minimality,
bounded uniqueness, and published representative-frontier observation membership. It is only the
first BCS R1 bootstrap
tranche, not the completed `BCS-v0` contract.

Still open:

- full finite fixture and algorithm contracts;
- abstract threshold-duality, monotonicity, frontier, and target-independent compiler theorems;
- a bridge receipt that maps each informal proposition to executable semantics and a formal theorem;
- an independent statement-fidelity review that did not author the definitions.

Statement-fidelity boundary:

- Lean can check that a proof term inhabits the encoded proposition under its kernel, imports, and
  axioms.
- Lean does not establish that the encoded proposition captures Aleph's intended natural-language
  claim, that the definitions measure the intended construct, or that a finite theorem generalizes to
  open model search.
- An independent reviewer must compare the informal claim, mathematical definitions, executable toy
  semantics, and formal statement before the result can support the paper.

Maintainer leaning: preserve and independently replay the merged bootstrap, then complete the finite
theory and statement map before an expensive autonomous-mathematics run. Neither the current
enumerator nor its Lean proofs imply a theorem about open model search.

### 12. Long-run compute program

Options:

- spend early compute on broad model brainstorming;
- spend it on exhaustive or solver-backed finite worlds;
- spend it on verifier-guided program/prompt evolution;
- reserve it for preregistered model and benchmark runs after the cheap oracles pass.

Maintainer leaning: use a resource ladder. First freeze the statement, candidate language, evaluator,
cheap baselines, and stop rule; then run finite enumeration or symbolic solvers; then small model search;
then matched-compute ablations; only then authorize long multi-agent or hosted runs. Use the six
`ResourceLedger/0.1.0` accounts---coordinate communication, decoder execution, search total work,
critical path, post-freeze verification, and model/interface description---without inventing parallel
budget taxonomies. Proposal calls, discovery-time evaluator calls, and retries remain typed children
of search work; human refinement remains provenance plus wall-clock metadata; token, time, and
financial views may be reported without pretending they share an additive unit.

### 13. Benchmark and hosted-runtime evidence

Open questions:

- Which release digest and model-identity evidence are required before a run may be called official?
- Which missing-output and provider-failure states remain in the denominator?
- What narrow pinned-run claim can a Kaggle receipt support after output readback, checksum
  verification, and offline replay?

Maintainer leaning: Aleph Bench's frozen repository artifacts define the scientific task. Kaggle is a
runtime and portability test; Hugging Face is a public dataset/result distribution surface; GitHub is
the code/governance entry. Platform success cannot define the construct, upgrade a diagnostic run to
scientific evidence, or replace item-level receipts and uncertainty.

## Questions to avoid for now

- Pricing, accounts, teams, or billing.
- Full model hosting strategy.
- Production security model.
- Public leaderboard claims before the benchmark protocol and evidence state are frozen.
- Strict Kolmogorov framing.

## How to settle open questions

Settle an open question only through a small implementation spike, an ADR, or a plan that names the
acceptance gate. Do not settle it silently inside UI code. Record active priority in
`docs/next-backlog.md`, durable path decisions in an ADR or checked-in plan, and review criteria in
`docs/quality-bar.md`. `docs/strategy.md` is historical fallback rationale.
